# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description
A simple interest calculator calculates the interest earned or paid on a principal amount over a specific period at a given annual interest rate.

## Formula
Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:
- **Principal (P):** Initial amount of money.
- **Rate (R):** Annual interest rate in percentage.
- **Time (T):** Time period in years.

## Example
- Principal: ₹10,000
- Rate: 5%
- Time: 2 years

Calculation:

Simple Interest = (10000 × 5 × 2) / 100

**Simple Interest = ₹1,000**

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

**Total Amount = ₹11,000**

C
